Instructions & conventions
Your skin lesion images

• You may use dermoscopic or clinical photographs. For clinical images, try to use a neutral background (e.g., plain skin or plain cloth) and avoid jewellery or text on the skin.

• Make sure the lesion is in sharp focus, without motion blur or strong flash glare. Natural or soft indoor lighting is preferred; avoid very dark or overexposed images.

• The lesion should roughly occupy 30–60% of the image area, centred in the frame with the whole lesion visible (do not crop off the edges).

• Remove obvious artefacts when possible (rulers, ink marks, heavy hair, air bubbles). A small number of artefacts is acceptable but may reduce model reliability.

• If you have multiple images of the same lesion, upload the one with the best focus and lighting.

These conventions help the AI model approximate the conditions of the research datasets. They do not replace professional dermoscopic imaging or clinical judgement.
